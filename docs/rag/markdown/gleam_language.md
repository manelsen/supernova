### Unofficial courses

-   [Exercism's Gleam track](https://exercism.org/tracks/gleam)

    Develop skills in Gleam and 70+ other languages with a unique blend
    of learning, practicing, and mentoring from skilled programmers. An
    educational non-profit and free forever.

-   [CodeCrafters](https://app.codecrafters.io/join?via=lpil)

    Practice writing complex software in Gleam and 20 other languages by
    implementing real-world systems such as Redis from scratch.

    *This is a referral link and a portion of any money paid will go to
    supporting Gleam development*.
